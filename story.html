Act I · Rewind

Roots

Paper boats on the pond. Bamboo-and-paper kites. Clay from the water's edge. Chalk on a slate. I didn't know it then, but I was learning to make things.

The slate became a frame. The kite became a cursor. The clay became a component. The tools changed. The instinct stayed.

Seq 11 Now · 2026

Designing with AI.
Building beyond the frame.

I work at the intersection of product design, UX strategy and technology.

From understanding complex problems to shaping interfaces and building working prototypes, I bring ideas closer to reality.

AI accelerates the process. Design judgment sets the direction.

What I bring to a product team.

  • Product thinkingUnderstanding complex problems, users, business requirements and product opportunities.
  • UX leadershipGuiding designers, facilitating collaboration and maintaining quality across product experiences.
  • Systems thinkingDesigning connected workflows, reusable components and scalable enterprise interfaces.
  • Design engineeringBridging design and implementation through prototyping, front-end exploration and AI-assisted development.

How I work

  1. Frame the problemUnderstand the users, requirements, constraints and business context before exploring solutions.
  2. Explore possibilitiesUse Figma, AI-assisted tools and rapid prototypes to explore different directions without committing too early.
  3. Apply judgmentCompare alternatives, challenge assumptions, evaluate usability and refine what matters.
  4. Make it workTurn the chosen direction into a working prototype or front-end experience, then test and refine it.
